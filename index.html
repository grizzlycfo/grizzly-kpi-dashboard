Key Insights & Recommended Actions
🔴
Revenue Timing vs. COGS Mismatch
January booked $896K COGS against $225K revenue. April booked $234K COGS against $223K revenue. Both months were deeply negative. Production spend is happening before (or instead of) revenue being recognized. Institute a hard rule: no production spend above 50% of contracted & signed revenue without CFO approval.
🔴
YTD Net Loss: -$206K
Despite $3.29M in revenue, Grizzly is $206K in the red through June. The underlying engine (Feb, Mar, May, Jun) IS profitable. The problem is two catastrophic months dragging the whole year. Better project financial controls would have made H1 profitable by ~$400K+.
🟡
Subcontractor Volatility Unresolved
Sub spend ranged from $64K (Apr) to $644K (Jan) across 6 months. Still no consistent pattern. This is the #1 margin risk. Every project needs a pre-signed subcontractor budget tied to client PO value before any work is authorized.
🟡
$275K Owner Distributions in a Loss Year
$275K paid out to owners through June while the business is -$206K net. June's $10K distribution is a good step. Recommend tying all future distributions to a rolling 60-day profitability test — if trailing 2-month NOI is negative, distributions pause.
🟡
Payroll Declining — Staff Restructuring?
Internal payroll dropped from $158K (Jan) to $34K (Jun) — a 78% reduction. This is dramatic. Either significant layoffs occurred or there's a classification shift from W2 to 1099. Worth verifying this is intentional and won't impact delivery capacity as revenue ramps.
🟢
May & June Show Real Recovery
May: +$174K net income. June: +$191K net income. Two consecutive profitable months at improving margins. If this holds through Q3, Grizzly could exit 2026 with a positive full-year result. The trend is definitively moving in the right direction.
🟢
H1 Total Revenue: $3.29M
$3.29M in 6 months puts Grizzly on pace for ~$6.5M annualized — a real agency at scale. The revenue is there. The challenge is purely margin discipline. If May/Jun margins hold (~39–58%), the business should generate $300–500K in H2 net income.
🟢
Operating Expenses Trending Down
Total OpEx dropped from $121K (Apr) to $58K (Jun) — a 52% reduction month-over-month. This is a significant improvement. Legal/consulting fees also dropped sharply in May/Jun. Keep monitoring to ensure this is structural and not just timing.
🟡
Revenue Concentration: Lumpy Projects
Revenue swings from $223K to $982K month-to-month. With no visible retainer base, cash flow is unpredictable. Target 40%+ of revenue from retainer/monthly contracts by end of 2026. Even $200K/month MRR would fundamentally change the risk profile.
